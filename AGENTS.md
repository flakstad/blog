# Blog Drafting Instructions

Before writing or editing blog posts in this repo, read:

`/Users/andreas/Projects/kari/kari/docs/content-tone-of-voice.md`

Use it as the drafting baseline even when the post is technical. Drafts should
sound like a person who has worked with the problem, not like marketing copy or
a generated article.

Idea notes may stay rough. For publishable prose, prefer concrete situations,
plain language, and a little dryness over polished "thought leadership" rhythm.
Avoid generic setup, dramatic one-line paragraphs, hard-selling, and repeated
summary sections.
