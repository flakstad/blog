## Breyta

I joined Breyta as its first employee and later became one of two lead engineers. Over roughly four and a half years, I worked across product development, frontend, backend, architecture, infrastructure, integrations and developer tooling.

The company built four substantially different product generations: a product-data-first CRM and data-activation product, AI-assisted qualitative research, continuous AI research, and an agent-first workflow and application platform. My work included significant parts of the research and chat experiences, reporting, the web application, the Breyta CLI, and the integration around agent-facing workflows and tools.

I was not the primary developer of either the original data-sync engine or the later workflow runtime. My role was broad product engineering around those systems: working out how technically ambitious capabilities should function for users and carrying them through implementation and production.

## Kari

Kari is an AI receptionist I built for Norwegian businesses. It is both an independent product and the source of roughly a year of practical production voice-AI experience.

Reliable appointment booking, changes and cancellations—sometimes in healthcare-related contexts—have required careful work on conversational state, purpose-built tools, monitoring, structured and sensitive input, and recovery from the failure modes that appear with real callers. This has been production engineering rather than a voice-agent demo.

## DNV Research

For roughly three years I worked in DNV’s research organisation on web products around FMU-based co-simulation, including Simulation Trust Center and some work related to Open Simulation Platform. My responsibility was mainly the applications surrounding the simulation technology, not the core simulation engine.

## Mobile banking at EVRY

My first job after university was building and operating 23 iOS mobile banking applications with more than 200,000 users in aggregate. A little over a year after joining, I became System Manager for iOS Mobile Banking, with technical and system responsibility rather than personnel management. I worked across iOS development, operations and production releases.
