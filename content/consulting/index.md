My work sits between product definition and implementation. I can help clarify an uncertain problem, make architecture and technology choices, and then build the software. I’m not looking to hand over a slide deck and leave the difficult parts to someone else.

## Situations where I’m useful

- Building a technically difficult product capability.
- Taking a prototype or early product into reliable production.
- Getting a stalled product or subsystem moving again.
- De-risking a rewrite, migration or major technical transition.
- Making architecture and technology decisions that need to hold up during implementation.
- Improving the reliability and testing of a critical system.

I work broadly as a product engineer, not as a generic AI consultant. That said, I have deep recent experience building LLM and agent systems for production: grounded research and reporting, retrieval, agent-facing tools and CLIs, sandboxed code execution, and agent-first product workflows.

Voice AI is another specialist area. Through building Kari I’ve worked on appointment workflows, conversational state, purpose-built tools, sensitive structured input, audio checks, stall monitoring, and the awkward failure modes that only appear with real callers. I’m interested in serious voice-AI work where reliability matters.

## A bounded first step

When the problem is uncertain, a **Technical Product Assessment** can be a useful first engagement. Over 5–8 working days, I inspect the product and relevant code, investigate the important technical and product risks, and run focused experiments or implementation spikes where they provide better evidence.

The result is a set of concrete findings and recommendations, with a realistic next implementation milestone. It is not a strategy workshop or a generic report. For Norwegian clients, a typical assessment is in the range of NOK 80,000–125,000 excluding VAT, depending on scope.

Not every engagement needs an assessment. Follow-on work can use fixed-price milestones, reserved weekly capacity, or time-based billing where uncertainty remains.
